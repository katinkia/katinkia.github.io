Sonar Duel

A daily naval deduction puzzle · iPhone & iPad

OverviewPrivacySupport

Battleship, with the luck removed. Sweep a region of the grid and the sonar tells you how many ship cells are inside it — never where they are. Cross-reference your sweeps, use the edge hints, and fire only when the logic says so. Every board can be solved without a single guess.

About this app

iOS (Swift / SwiftUI), for iPhone and iPad. A new fair board every day, with streaks and a spoiler-free share card, plus practice boards in several sizes. Everything runs on your device: no account, no sign-in, no ads. Sonar Duel Pro is a one-time unlock for unlimited practice, every board size and difficulty, and extra board themes. The daily puzzle is free forever.
