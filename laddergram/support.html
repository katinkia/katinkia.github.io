How the daily puzzle works

Everyone gets the same ladder each day, and the day rolls over at midnight in your own time zone. Climb from the start word to the goal by changing one letter at a time, and every rung has to be a real word. Par is the score a good solver is expected to make, so a perfect climb finishes one under par.

Purchases & Laddergram Pro

Laddergram Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. It unlocks every difficulty, unlimited practice, the full archive and deeper stats.

If a purchase didn't unlock, or you're setting up a new device, open Laddergram's Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they carry across your devices at no extra cost. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Laddergram plays fully offline with no account. Your puzzles, streak and stats live on your device only. See the privacy policy for the detail.
